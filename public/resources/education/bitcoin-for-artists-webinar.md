## Who this is for

Artists, performers, writers, and creators who want a **Bitcoin-only**, practical introduction focused on:
- Getting paid globally
- Basic self-custody and security habits
- Pricing in sats and managing volatility
- Avoiding common mistakes and scams

This is educational content, not financial, legal, or tax advice.

## Learning goals

By the end, you should be able to:
- Explain (in plain language) why Bitcoin is different from “fintech money”
- Choose a safe path to receive and hold bitcoin
- Know what *not* to do (custody pitfalls, impersonation scams)
- Use simple habits for pricing, invoices, and record-keeping

## 1) Why artists care: the “artist problem” in one page

Artists often face:
- **Irregular income** and long gaps between paychecks
- **Gatekeepers** (platform rules, payout limits, deplatforming risk)
- **Inflation** (time and savings get diluted)
- **Cross-border friction** (fees, delays, account freezes)

Bitcoin helps because it is:
- **Permissionless**: anyone can receive it
- **Censorship-resistant**: harder to block than traditional rails
- **Portable**: value can move across borders with you
- **Fixed-supply**: no central party can print more

## 2) Getting paid in Bitcoin (simple flows)

### Option A — easiest: receive to a wallet address

1. Install a reputable Bitcoin wallet (mobile is OK to start).
2. Generate a receive address.
3. Share the address (or QR) with a patron.
4. Verify the payment is received.

**Best practice:** always verify the address on your own device before sharing.

### Option B — professional: invoices (BTCPay Server)

Invoices help when you need:
- A clean payment link
- A single “checkout” for multiple patrons
- Better tracking and receipts

Resource: **BTCPay setup guide**  
https://bitcoinforthearts.org/resources/btcpay-setup-guide.md

## 3) Custody basics (keep it safe without paranoia)

### Key terms (plain language)
- **Wallet**: an app/device that holds the keys to spend your bitcoin.
- **Seed phrase**: the backup words. Whoever has them can take the funds.
- **Self-custody**: you control the keys.

### Safer defaults
- Start with a small amount on mobile while learning.
- For meaningful savings, move to a dedicated setup (hardware wallet / multisig).

### What not to do
- Don’t screenshot your seed phrase.
- Don’t store seed words in cloud notes/docs.
- Don’t share seed words with anyone, ever.
- Don’t trust “support” DMs asking for wallet info.

## 4) Pricing in sats (practical approach)

Many artists prefer one of these approaches:
- **USD price + BTC at checkout**: simple for most customers
- **Sats-based pricing** for Bitcoin-native patrons

If you price in sats:
- Decide a “refresh cadence” (daily/weekly) to adjust for volatility
- Be explicit: “Prices in sats may adjust weekly”
- Keep records of conversions for bookkeeping

## 5) Record-keeping (make tax time less painful)

At minimum, track:
- Date received
- Amount in sats/BTC
- USD reference price (at time received)
- Purpose (commission, ticket, donation, etc.)

Resource: **Donor receipt template**  
https://bitcoinforthearts.org/resources/donor-receipt-template.md

## 6) Talking about Bitcoin without cringe

Good messaging is:
- Clear: “Bitcoin-only. No NFTs. No token launches.”
- Practical: “Here’s how you can pay and what to expect.”
- Respectful: don’t overpromise, don’t shame people.

Suggested line:
> “We use Bitcoin because it’s the most durable, permissionless money for supporting uncensorable creativity.”
