# Sovereign Circle Membership Program — Summary for Trustees

**Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc.**
February 2026

---

## Overview

We are transitioning our monthly donor program from a traditional donation-with-perks model to a membership model called the **Sovereign Circle**. This shift reframes the relationship from transactional giving to community belonging. Members don't just donate — they join a community, connect with the artists they help fund, participate in grantmaking decisions, and unlock deeper access the longer they stay.

The core insight driving this change: for people to commit monthly alongside all their other subscriptions, BFTA needs to feel like a membership they belong to, not another bill they're paying.

---

## Membership Levels

All levels are available as monthly or annual commitments. Annual pricing is the same rate (no discount) — we are asking for support, not selling a product.

| Level | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Satoshi Supporter | $5/mo | $60/yr |
| Orange Piller Patron | $11/mo | $132/yr |
| Hard Cap Hero | $21/mo | $252/yr |
| Sovereign Stacker | $51/mo | $612/yr |
| Renaissance Guardian | $101/mo | $1,212/yr |

**Benefits stack:** every level includes all benefits from the levels below it.

## Five Pillars of Membership

1. **The Commons** — A members-only community space (Signal/Telegram) for connecting with artists, fellow members, and the BFTA team. Behind-the-scenes updates, candid org discussions, direct access.

2. **The Gallery** — Quarterly digital art drops from grant recipients, exclusive to active members. An annual Sovereign Collection — a curated digital portfolio of all works created with member funding that year.

3. **The Voice** — Members vote on the quarterly Audience Choice grant. $21+ members can choose which art discipline their support is weighted toward. Annual member surveys shape organizational priorities.

4. **The Ledger** — Public member recognition on the Sovereign Circle page (opt-in). On-chain impact tracking. Member acknowledgment in the annual transparency report.

5. **The Journey** — Tenure milestones that reward staying:

| Milestone | Status | Permanent? |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Welcome (kit, Commons access, member listing) | No |
| 3 months | Steady Supporter (badge, quarterly impact reports) | No |
| 6 months | Art Connection (first quarterly art drop) | No |
| 12 months | Annual Member (name in transparency report, Sovereign Collection) | Report mention is permanent |
| 24 months | Legacy Member (permanent recognition, even after cancellation) | Yes |

Annual members immediately unlock the 3-month Steady Supporter milestone.

## Payment Options

Every level offers two payment methods:

- **Traditional payment** (Stripe) — Card, Apple Pay, etc. Monthly memberships auto-renew. Annual memberships renew yearly.
- **Bitcoin** (BTCPay Server) — BTC on-chain and Lightning. Since Bitcoin doesn't support auto-recurring payments, BTC members return each month to renew (or pay annually for a single transaction). Membership payments are tagged in the system and distinguishable from one-time donations.

---

## Discipline Allocation ($21+ members)

Members at the Hard Cap Hero level and above have the option to direct their support toward a specific art discipline: visual arts, music, theater, dance, writing, or film. This is handled via email (members reply to their welcome email or write to donate@bitcoinforthearts.org) rather than through an automated system. It is an option, not a requirement.

This approach avoids the risks of letting members fund specific individual artists (popularity contests, governance complications) while still creating personal investment in the mission.

## Member Recognition Policy

- All active members are listed on the Sovereign Circle page (opt-in, with consent)
- $51+ members receive a featured card (name, optional message, optional link)
- $101+ members receive a spotlight profile (photo, quote, link)
- Members who cancel before 24 months are removed from the page
- Members who complete 24 continuous months earn permanent Legacy Member status
- Newsletter shoutouts are milestone-based (at signup, 3mo, 6mo, 12mo, 24mo), not every month

## Operational Cost

The program is designed to run on near-zero marginal cost:

- The Commons: free group chat, light moderation
- The Gallery: digital files from artists already producing work
- The Voice: quarterly poll/form
- The Ledger: one page on the website, maintained automatically
- The Journey: milestone emails triggered by dates in the database
- Welcome kits: ~$3-5 per sticker pack, US-only, one-time per member

No physical art fulfillment, no expensive merchandise, no event logistics required.

## Tax Status

Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit. Membership contributions may be tax-deductible as allowed by law. Receipts are provided automatically through Stripe. BTCPay donation records are stored and available on request.
