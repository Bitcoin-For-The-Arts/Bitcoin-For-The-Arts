=== TITLE ===

Share Your Bitcoin Journey: Episode 4 – Benjamin Ella's Sovereign Leap from The Royal Ballet to Bitcoin


=== DESCRIPTION ===

In this powerful episode, Bitcoin For The Arts founder Dion Wilson sits down with Benjamin Ella — a Soloist of The Royal Ballet for 17 seasons — for a conversation that could only happen between two professional dancers who found Bitcoin.

Benjamin shares his journey from Melbourne tennis courts to London's Royal Opera House, creating roles for choreographers like Wayne McGregor, Crystal Pite, and Hofesh Shechter. After retiring in December 2025, he's now building a new chapter rooted in choreography, faith, family, and sound money.

Two dancers. Two of the world's most prestigious companies. One shared conviction: Bitcoin is the path forward for the arts.

IN THIS EPISODE:
- Growing up in a ballet family and the economics of running a dance school
- From tennis player to Royal Ballet Soloist — the full Melbourne-to-London story
- Overcoming a career-threatening injury (titanium pins, 3 years of setbacks)
- Discovering Bitcoin during COVID while serving as a union representative
- How negotiating dancer contracts revealed the broken economics of arts funding
- The moment the "veil was lifted" — Robert Breedlove, Michael Saylor, The Bitcoin Standard
- Why Bitcoin led him to Christianity and how Biblical principles deepen his conviction
- Arts Council funding cuts: from 50%+ to 19% and falling
- Christopher Wheeldon's new production company and how Bitcoin could reshape dance funding
- Trying to orange-pill The Royal Ballet (twice) — and what comes next
- Building a Bitcoin treasury for his church and his family
- The vision: a choreography studio in London running on a Bitcoin standard
- Advice for young dancers: "Don't ignore Bitcoin at your own peril"

ABOUT BENJAMIN ELLA:
Benjamin Ella danced with The Royal Ballet from 2009 to 2025, performing principal and soloist roles including Albrecht (Giselle), The Prince (Swan Lake), Hans-Peter/Nutcracker, and Benvolio (Romeo and Juliet). He created roles in Flight Pattern (Crystal Pite), The Dante Project (Wayne McGregor), Woolf Works, Untouchable, and more. Winner of the 2008 Youth America Grand Prix silver medal and joint Outstanding Graduate of The Royal Ballet School. He is now pursuing choreography, teaching, and expanding his family's ballet training program across 14 countries.

Royal Ballet profile: https://www.rbo.org.uk/people/benjamin-ella
