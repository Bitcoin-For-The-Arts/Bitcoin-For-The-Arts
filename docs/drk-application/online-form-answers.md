## Short Answer Fields (adapt to character limits)

### Mission (1–2 sentences)

Bitcoin For The Arts, Inc. empowers independent artists with Bitcoin micro-grants, financial literacy workshops, and censorship-resistant tools — building the first nonprofit dedicated to paying creators exclusively in Bitcoin and establishing a long-term Bitcoin reserve for perpetual arts funding.

### Problem (1–2 sentences)

Independent artists face compounding crises of financial precarity, platform censorship, and exclusion from institutional funding — with the top 2% of arts nonprofits receiving over 60% of philanthropic arts dollars, while working artists earn a median of $35,000/year in depreciating currency with no path to financial sovereignty.

### Solution (1–2 sentences)

BFTA provides direct Bitcoin grants ($500–$25,000), hands-on workshops on self-custody and value-for-value models, and onboarding to censorship-resistant platforms — combining immediate financial support with the tools and education artists need to build permanently independent careers.

### How is your approach unique? (1–2 sentences)

BFTA is the first nonprofit paying artists exclusively in Bitcoin, with a radical transparency model (all governance documents published openly) and a 55/30/10/5 allocation framework that includes a Bitcoin endowment designed to appreciate over time — creating a self-sustaining funding base that grows rather than erodes.

### Target population

Independent artists worldwide, with initial grant funding for U.S.-based projects. Priority populations include: economically precarious working artists, creators facing platform censorship or deplatforming, and artists in regions with restrictive financial systems who cannot access traditional funding channels.

### Results to date

Incorporated Q4 2025; launched grant application system (processing begins Q3 2026); published 8+ in-depth artist stories from 6 countries; produced 3 open-licensed video episodes; launched 5-tier Sovereign Circle membership program; established 5-member founding board; published full governance framework (bylaws, ethics, endowment policy); built open-source technology platform with BTCPay Server integration.

### How do you plan to scale?

Three phases: (1) Foundation (2025–2026): launch first grant cycles at $500–$2,000, build community, establish governance. (2) Growth (2027–2028): scale grants from $500 to $10,000, launch residency and education pilots ($20K–$50K/year each), expand board to 7–9 members. (3) Sustainability (2029+): scale grants to $25K, reach 1 BTC reserve target, open-source the model for replication.

### Revenue model / financial sustainability

Individual Bitcoin and fiat donations via BTCPay Server and Stripe; Sovereign Circle recurring memberships (5 tiers, $5–$101/month); institutional grants; workshop fees (sliding scale); HODL Vault Bitcoin endowment (5% allocation) that appreciates in purchasing power over time, creating a structurally self-sustaining funding base.

### How would DRK's support help your organization?

DRK's unrestricted three-year funding would enable BFTA to fund our first 50+ artist grants and launch our residency and education pilots. DRK's board service model would provide critical governance, fundraising, and scaling expertise at our most important inflection point — the transition from founding infrastructure to operational grant-making. Access to DRK's portfolio network of 280+ social enterprises would accelerate partnerships and learning.

## Important Notes

1. **DRK requires a standard resume/CV** for the founder — not a text bio. Prepare a traditional PDF resume for Dion Wilson.
