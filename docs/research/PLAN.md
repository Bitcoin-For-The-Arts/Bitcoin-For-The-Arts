# Research Plan

## Phase 0 - Problem framing and constraints

- Define the research question and success criteria.
- Capture assumptions, dependencies, and known unknowns.
- Gather baseline context, references, and existing evidence.
- Produce a short brief that scopes what will and will not be researched.

## Phase 1 - Discovery

- Identify primary and secondary sources.
- Collect qualitative and quantitative data points.
- Track source quality and confidence levels.

## Phase 2 - Synthesis

- Group findings into themes.
- Resolve conflicts between sources and note unresolved gaps.
- Draft key insights with supporting evidence.

## Phase 3 - Recommendations

- Turn insights into prioritized recommendations.
- Call out risks, trade-offs, and follow-up research items.
- Publish final notes and decision support artifacts.
